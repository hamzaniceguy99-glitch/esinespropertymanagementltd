Program 01 · Explained, not just issued

Service charges & accounts

Budgets are built line by line with the directors rather than uplifted by a percentage. Demands are issued with the statutory information leaseholders are entitled to, client monies are held in designated client accounts, and year-end accounts reconcile to the budget with variances explained.

Repairs & maintenance

Program 03 · Done in the right order

Section 20 & compliance

Qualifying works and long-term agreements require consultation under section 20 of the Landlord and Tenant Act 1985, and getting the order wrong can limit what's recoverable. We run the notices properly, arrange buildings insurance, and keep fire risk assessments and other safety obligations on a schedule.
